Beyond the R1s

05 · How they compute

Conventional HPC at lower volume — not a different kind of user.

Non-R1 PIs are not a small-job, interactive population. Their jobs look like R1 jobs — mostly 9–256 cores, mostly running 18+ hours — but they land on the general-purpose CPU systems and skip the GPU and AI resources. Gateways carry almost none of their usage; one gateway, ChemCompute at Sonoma State, accounts for 95% of non-R1 gateway jobs.

Non-R1 usage dips in January–February and peaks May–July and again in November, consistent with faculty at teaching-heavy institutions computing between semesters. Disciplinary mix differs too: chemistry is 16% of non-R1 ACEs versus 9% for R1s, and non-R1 PIs are under-represented in computer science (21% of non-R1 PIs vs. 33% of R1 PIs). Geographically, New York, California and New Jersey dominate non-R1 volume; Puerto Rico has 12 active non-R1 PIs and no R1 usage at all.

07 · What would move it

Three interventions the data points at.

Activation

Call the awarded-never-used list.

non-R1 academic institutions hold a current award and have never run a job. They have already cleared the hardest hurdle. A first-job concierge — login, environment, one submitted batch script — is the cheapest usage ACCESS can buy.

Persistence

Support the first project through its second semester.

Non-R1 four-quarter retention runs against for R1s, and of non-designated colleges use compute for three months or fewer. Scheduled check-ins at months 3 and 9 of an Explore award, timed to the January and summer dips, target the drop-off directly.

Growth

Nudge sustained Explore users into Discover.

of non-R1 projects are Explore and only are Maximize. PIs with usage in six of the last twelve months are the graduation candidates; a templated Discover request and a resource-fit conversation (most are on Expanse and Bridges-2 RM) turns participation into volume.

Method and caveats

Usage is from the ACCESS XDMoD Jobs realm, to , grouped by PI institution ( institutions, B ACEs, reconciling exactly to XDMoD's unfiltered total). Institutions were matched to the Carnegie 2025 Research Activity Designations public data file by normalized name, with manual overrides (data/overrides.json) for campus-system naming (e.g. "University of Michigan" → Ann Arbor); every R1 match was hand-checked. Units of R1s (NCSA, PSC, SDSC, TACC, medical schools, observatories) were folded into R1. Institutions not in the Carnegie research file were classed as other academic, non-academic (labs, industry, hospitals, government, nonprofits) or foreign/unknown by name. Non-academic PIs (2.4% of usage) are shown for completeness but excluded from "non-R1 academic".

Awarded projects are the current projects in the ACCESS Allocations public feed at the time of the pull, with awarded credits summed from ACCESS Credits line items. "Never used" means the institution has no XDMoD job record at all in the four-year window; the R1 figure is mostly name variants and should be read as noise. HBCU and tribal-college flags are from IPEDS HD2023 via UNITID; IPEDS carries no HSI flag. Retention counts an institution as active in a quarter if any job was charged; partial quarters are excluded from quarterly charts. Program years run September–August.
